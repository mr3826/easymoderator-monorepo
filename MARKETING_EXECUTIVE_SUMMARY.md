## 🎯 THE OPPORTUNITY

EasyModerator is a **production-grade SaaS platform** solving a **₳100K+ pain point** for Bangladesh ecommerce sellers:

> **Problem:** Shop owners handle 100–500 customer messages daily, losing sales, sleep, and ₳5000+/month to hiring staff + fraud

> **Solution:** AI automates 80% of conversations in 48 hours, with local expertise (RTO fraud detection, Pathao integration)

> **Market:** 50K+ ecommerce shops in Bangladesh × ৳8000–15000 annual savings = **₳400–750 Cr TAM**

### 1️⃣ REPOSITIONING (Weeks 1–2)

**Why?** Unlocks expansion beyond Facebook shops → WhatsApp, Instagram, Marketplace sellers

**Expected Lift:** +20% trial signup conversion rate

### 2️⃣ CONTENT & COMMUNITY MOAT (Months 1–3)
**Goal:** Build community-powered growth (reduce CAC, increase referrals)

| Channel | Action | Target (90 Days) | Cost |
|---------|--------|-------------------|------|
| **Blog** | 5-part series on automation, RTO, revenue growth | 1500 views/post | ৳10K |

**Expected Lift:** 30% of new signups from community by month 3

### 3️⃣ CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION & PAID GROWTH (Months 2–3)
**Goal:** Prove unit economics before scaling paid spend

| Initiative | Timeline | Expected Lift |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| **Headline A/B test** | Week 1 | +15–20% CTR |
| **Homepage CTA optimization** | Week 1 | +10% conversions |
| **Schema markup** (SEO) | Week 1 | +20% organic CTR |
| **Paid campaigns** (Facebook + Google) | Weeks 5–8 | +200 trial signups if CAC < ৳1500 |

**Expected Outcome:** By week 8, CAC drops from ৳2000 → ৳1500, enabling scale
