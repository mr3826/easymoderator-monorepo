**Last Updated:** December 1, 2024

## 1. Introduction

Easy Moderator ("we," "our," or "us") is a business messaging and moderation platform designed to help businesses manage their customer communications across various messaging platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our service.

This policy is compliant with Bangladesh Digital Security Act, 2018 and international data protection standards including GDPR principles where applicable.

### 2.2 Business Information

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Localization
- All Bangladesh user data is stored on servers located within Bangladesh
- Data processing complies with local data protection requirements
- Backup systems maintain data integrity and availability

### 4.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Strict access controls and authentication systems
- **Regular Audits:** Security audits and vulnerability assessments
- **Employee Training:** Staff trained on data protection and security

### 4.3 Data Retention
- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Conversation Data:** Retained for 2 years, then securely deleted
- **Analytics Data:** Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days
- **Deleted Accounts:** Data permanently deleted within 30 days of deletion request
